About KISSA MATCHA

Where Centuries
Meet the Present

Deep in the valleys of Uji, Kyoto, morning mist drifts over tea bushes that have been tended by the same families for generations. This is where KISSA MATCHA begins.

We work directly with a single-estate garden, harvesting only the first flush of young leaves each spring — Ichibancha — to capture the highest concentration of L-theanine and umami.

Granite stone mills turn at 30 rpm, slowly enough to preserve colour, fragrance, and every nutrient. The result: matcha of rare depth and vivid emerald brilliance.

The Sensory Profile

A Cup That
Speaks in Layers

01

Aroma

香り

Fresh cut grass, warm spring earth, and a whisper of ocean air — the unmistakable first breath of ceremonial matcha.

02

Bitterness

苦味

A clean, bright edge that awakens without harshness. The bitterness of KISSA MATCHA is the bitterness of life at its most alive.

Our Philosophy

Ancient Ritual.
Modern Life.

The Japanese tea ceremony, Chanoyu, is not about drinking. It is about presence — the radical act of being fully here, in this moment, with this bowl.

KISSA MATCHA was founded on the belief that this philosophy has never been more necessary. We live in an age of constant noise. The morning bowl is a counterpoint: a few minutes of intention, craft, and quiet.

We do not ask you to adopt a ceremony. We invite you to create your own. A cold matcha at your desk. A whisked bowl before dawn. A latte shared with someone you love.

Whatever your ritual, KISSA MATCHA is there — pure, precise, and exactly what it needs to be.
